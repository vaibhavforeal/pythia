Questions people actually ask

I don't know my exact birth time.

Use 12:00 — the app suggests it. Most of your chart is stable across a whole day: your Moon sign, your nakshatra, and where the planets sit barely move. What does shift is your rising sign and which house each planet lands in, so treat those two as approximate until you find the real time. A birth certificate or hospital record usually has it.

Is it actually free?

Yes. No card, no trial, nothing to cancel. Everything described on this page is free to use.

Why is my sign different from the one I've always known?

Because Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, which tracks the constellations where they actually sit now. The Western one uses a fixed zodiac that has drifted roughly 24° over the last two thousand years. In practice your Vedic sign is usually the one before your familiar one. Nothing is broken — it's a different measurement, and it's the one this entire system is built on.

Why do I need an account?

So your chart, your saved people and your chats stay yours and are waiting when you come back, instead of you re-typing your birth details every visit. It's free and it's one tap with Google.

Do I have to believe in astrology?

No. Plenty of people use it as a way to look at a decision from an angle they wouldn't have picked themselves. It's a guidance tool, not fixed fate — and for anything legal, financial or medical, talk to a qualified professional.
